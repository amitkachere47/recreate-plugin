What your free scan shows

Scans are done on a Karada Scan body composition monitor.

  • Weight and BMI
  • Body fat %
  • Visceral (belly) fat
  • Muscle mass
  • Metabolic age
  • Body water / hydration
  • Basal metabolic rate (calories at rest)

Frequently asked questions

Is the body scan really free?

Yes. The first body composition scan and consultation are free with no obligation.

How long does the scan take?

About 20 minutes including a short chat about your goals.

Do I need to prepare?

Come with an empty stomach or 2–3 hours after a light meal, and avoid heavy exercise before the scan.

Is this medical treatment?

No. We provide general wellness and nutrition coaching. Please consult a doctor for medical conditions.
